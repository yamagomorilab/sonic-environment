What it is and how to use it

A colour-to-timbre sonification of 3D terrain. Rain drops sample the surface, wind agents perturb their trajectories, and impacts are quantised to a step sequencer.

  1. Press Start (or Space) and listen: the weather arc changes on its own.
  2. Rotate, pan and zoom the scene with the mouse.
  3. Load a .glb file to use the colours of a real territory.
  4. In Active colours: click to mute, double click to hear a single colour.
  5. Move wind and rain for manual control. Record saves the audio.

The arc starts when you press Start. If you move wind, rain or direction by hand, it pauses and you take manual control.
Active colours
Click: mute or unmute a colour. Double click: hear only that colour. Muted colours stay visible but darker in the scene. With a GLB, the colours are found in the model.

Geological bass
Cm7 → Ebmaj7 → Fm7 → Gm7. Terrain roughness, the colour hit and the wind open the filter and add ghost notes. With triplets the bass switches to a shuffle pattern.
